# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DECA students practicing prepared events on their own, usually on a laptop after school. They need a calm workspace that turns a draft into specific, rubric-aware next steps without writing the project for them.

## Product Purpose

DECA Draftroom helps students review written projects, compare their work to event expectations, and build a focused revision plan. Success means a student can move from uncertainty to a short list of high-impact improvements in one session.

## Brand Personality

Focused, candid, encouraging. The product should feel like a sharp advisor's desk, not a generic AI chat window or a competition leaderboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid gamified dashboards, noisy SaaS gradients, fake precision, and presenting unverified student submissions as a public corpus. Do not bypass DECA+ access controls.

## Design Principles

- Show the reasoning behind feedback.
- Coach the writer; never replace the writer.
- Make the next revision obvious.
- Treat source provenance as part of the product experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use keyboard-visible focus, semantic controls, strong contrast, readable measures, and a reduced-motion path. Never rely on color alone for score or status.
