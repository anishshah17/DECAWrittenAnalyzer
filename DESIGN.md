# Design

## Direction

Focused studio: warm paper surface, ink-black type, cobalt actions, and quiet red-pencil signals for revision. Product UI should feel editorial and tactile without becoming decorative.

## Tokens

- Surface: warm paper `oklch(97% 0.018 82)`
- Ink: `oklch(22% 0.02 75)`
- Muted ink: `oklch(54% 0.025 75)`
- Panel: `oklch(94% 0.018 82)`
- Cobalt action: `oklch(49% 0.17 258)`
- Revision red: `oklch(57% 0.17 25)`
- Radius: 12px controls, 18px panels
- Shadow: `0 18px 50px oklch(35% 0.02 70 / 0.08)`

## Typography

Use Geist for the interface. Keep labels compact and prose readable at 65–75ch. Use weight and spacing for hierarchy, not display type.

## Components

Use a compact top bar, segmented workspace navigation, bordered paper panels, event chips, rubric bars, and action buttons with visible focus states.
